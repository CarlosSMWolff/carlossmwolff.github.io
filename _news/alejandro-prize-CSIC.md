My first PhD student, Alejandro Vivas Viaña, has been awarded another prize for [his PhD thesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10672), this time the [IV Premio Tesis Doctoral Relevante del CSIC](https://eventos.corp.csic.es/event/150/) by CSIC. I was honored to collect the prize on his behalf at the awards ceremony, that took place on September 7th in Serrano.

Congratulations, Alejandro!
