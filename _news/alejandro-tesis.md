Alejandro Vivas Viaña has successfully defended [his PhD thesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.10672) *Nonclassical Driven-dissipative dynamics in Collective Quantum Optics* (my first one as supervisor), at Universidad Autónoma de Madrid. 

For the jury members, we had a lineup of great quantum optics experts, both theorist and experimentalist:
- [Johannes Feist](https://johannesfeist.eu/) (UAM)
- [Tomás Ramos](https://quinfog.hbar.es/members/tomas-ramos/) (IFF-CSIC)
- [Carlos Antón Solanas](https://www.antonsolanaslab.com/) (UAM)
- [Erik Gauger](https://qtt.eps.hw.ac.uk/) (Heriot-Watt)
- [Kai Müller](https://www.professoren.tum.de/en/mueller-kai) (TUM)

As a supervisor, I am extremely proud and happy about the defence, the incredibly beautiful manuscript crafted by Alejandro, and most of all, his development as a scientist during the last five years. Godspeed, Alejandro!
